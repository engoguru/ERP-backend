# NGO Guru Pvt. Ltd. - Company Overview

NGO Guru Pvt. Ltd. is a consultancy and support organization that helps NGOs, Trusts, Societies, Section 8 Companies, and social enterprises across India achieve regulatory compliance, sustainable growth, and operational excellence.

Our mission is to empower social organizations through compliance support, fundraising strategies, CSR guidance, technology solutions, and capacity-building programs. Our vision is to become India's most trusted partner for NGO growth and development.

The organization employs approximately 50–100 professionals across consultancy, compliance, finance, CSR advisory, technology, digital marketing, client support, and operations.

### Core Services

* NGO, Trust, Society & Section 8 Registration
* 12A, 80G & FCRA Consultancy
* CSR Funding Support & Proposal Writing
* Grant Writing & Fundraising Strategy
* Audit & Compliance Support
* Website Development & Donation Portals
* Crowdfunding Solutions
* CRM & ERP Development
* Digital Marketing & SEO

### Presence

NGO Guru serves organizations across India and conducts seminars in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Patna, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Lucknow, and Pune.

### Key Highlight

The organization hosts the annual NGO Growth Mahakumbh in Delhi, bringing together NGO leaders, CSR professionals, donors, and social entrepreneurs to discuss funding, compliance, technology, and organizational growth.

# Employee Policies

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to maintain honesty, professionalism, integrity, and respect while interacting with colleagues, clients, and stakeholders. Confidential company and client information must not be shared without authorization.

## Office Rules

The company follows a six-day working week. Employees should maintain a professional work environment, follow reporting guidelines, and use company resources responsibly. Unauthorized absence or repeated violation of company rules may result in disciplinary action.

## Professional Behavior

Employees should demonstrate teamwork, accountability, and a positive attitude. Harassment, discrimination, abusive language, or unprofessional conduct is not permitted. Employees are encouraged to communicate respectfully and support organizational goals.

## Leave Approval Process

Employees are entitled to one casual leave per month and two short leaves of up to two hours each. Leave requests should be submitted to the reporting manager in advance. Emergency leave cases should be communicated as soon as possible.

## Attendance Expectations

Employees are expected to report between 9:30 AM and 9:40 AM. Attendance after 9:40 AM is considered late. Standard office closing time is 6:30 PM. Consistent punctuality and attendance are expected from all employees.

## Employee Benefits

Benefits include festival holidays such as Holi and Diwali, annual employee trips, professional learning opportunities, and a collaborative work environment. Currently, no PF or medical deductions are applied to employee salaries.

## Performance Expectations

Employees are expected to complete assigned tasks on time, maintain quality standards, achieve performance goals, and contribute to team success. Regular performance reviews may be conducted by management.

## Travel Policy

Employees may be required to travel for seminars, workshops, client meetings, training programs, or company events. Official travel must be approved by management and conducted in a professional manner.

# Leave Policy

## Casual Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to **one casual leave per month**. Casual leave should be requested in advance whenever possible and is subject to manager approval. Employees are encouraged to plan their leave to avoid disruption to business operations.

## Short Leave Policy

Employees may avail **two short leaves per month**, with each short leave allowing up to **two hours** of absence. Short leaves may be taken either in the morning or evening and should be communicated to the reporting manager before use.

## Annual Leave Process

Employees should submit leave requests through the designated approval process. Planned leave should be applied for in advance to ensure proper workload management and team coordination.

## Leave Approval Workflow

1. Employee submits leave request.
2. Reporting manager reviews the request.
3. Approval or rejection is communicated to the employee.
4. Approved leave is recorded in attendance records.

Emergency leave requests should be communicated as soon as possible.

## Holiday Calendar

The company follows a holiday calendar announced by management each year. Additional holidays may be declared based on organizational requirements.

## Festival Holidays

The company observes major festival holidays including:

* Holi

Additional festival holidays may be announced by management.
